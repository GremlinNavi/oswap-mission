# Contributing to the OSWAP Mission Repository

Contributions that improve clarity, accessibility, consistency, evidence, or governance of the OSWAP mission documentation are welcome.

## Good contribution targets

Useful contributions include:

- clarifying ambiguous mission language without changing its meaning;
- identifying contradictions between mission documents;
- improving accessibility and plain-language readability;
- proposing multilingual or localization guidance;
- documenting public-interest implications with appropriate evidence;
- tightening scope boundaries or correcting overclaims;
- improving governance and change-control language; and
- identifying places where implementation claims should be separated from mission goals.

## Substantive mission changes

A proposal that changes OSWAP's stated purpose, public-interest commitments, or scope should explain:

1. the current wording;
2. the proposed wording;
3. why the change is needed;
4. which documents and OSWAP components may be affected; and
5. whether the change is editorial, policy-level, or a new mission direction.

## Evidence and claims

Do not present planned features, hypotheses, social benefits, legal interpretations, certifications, endorsements, or deployment outcomes as established facts without evidence.

## Accessibility

Prefer readable headings, concise paragraphs, descriptive links, plain-text-compatible structure, and terminology that can be understood without unnecessary jargon. Technical precision should be retained where it matters.
