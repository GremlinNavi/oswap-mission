# OSWAP Mission Statement

Status: Draft v0.1

## Mission

The Open-Source World Access Project (OSWAP) exists to make digital access, software preservation, AI-assisted computing, and auditable automation more accessible, portable, interoperable, and accountable.

OSWAP develops open standards, reference implementations, and documentation that help people express intent in human-readable forms while preserving deterministic validation, explicit consent, provenance, independent verification, privacy minimization, and provider-agnostic operation.

## Purpose

OSWAP treats software and digital infrastructure as public-interest systems whose usability should not depend unnecessarily on one vendor, one interface, one language, one model provider, one repository host, or one class of technically privileged user.

The project therefore explores ways to make advanced digital workflows easier to understand and operate without weakening the execution boundary that separates interpretation from authorization and action.

## Commitments

OSWAP aims to:

1. improve accessibility through readable interfaces, multilingual support, typo tolerance, plain-language previews, and deterministic fallback paths;
2. preserve meaningful human control through explicit consent, effect classification, authorization checks, and cancellation;
3. make consequential actions auditable through provenance, integrity evidence, before/after state, and independent verification;
4. support local-first and privacy-preserving operation where practical;
5. remain portable across models, providers, hosts, repositories, operating systems, and compatible transport layers;
6. support preservation and resilience through reproducible records, open formats, replication, and independently verifiable copies;
7. minimize unnecessary disclosure of personal identity while preserving accountable attribution;
8. prefer open, documented, interoperable interfaces over unnecessary platform lock-in; and
9. distinguish evidence, integrity, interpretation, and authorization rather than collapsing them into a single automated decision.

## Public-interest orientation

OSWAP is developed as an open-source public-interest project. This statement describes project purpose; it does not assert a particular incorporated, charitable, governmental, standards-body, or other legal status.

## Human authority

AI systems may assist with interpretation, research, translation, coding, or workflow preparation, but model output is not treated as automatic authorization. Consequential execution should remain subject to deterministic validation, applicable permissions, and human-controlled policy boundaries.

## International orientation

OSWAP is intended to support localization and region-specific deployment without making one national implementation, language, or hosting provider the universal definition of the project. Shared semantics should remain portable even when presentation, infrastructure, and local policy differ.

## Versioning

Material changes to this mission should be explicit, reviewable, and versioned. Historical mission text should remain available where practical so changes in project purpose can be audited over time.
