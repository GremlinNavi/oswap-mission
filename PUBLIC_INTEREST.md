# OSWAP and Public Interest

OSWAP is designed around the idea that digital infrastructure has social consequences. Interfaces, identity requirements, platform dependencies, language assumptions, audit practices, and preservation policies can determine who is able to participate and who must rely on intermediaries.

## Intended impact

OSWAP research aims to explore whether open and auditable workflow infrastructure can:

- lower technical barriers without making consequential execution opaque or uncontrolled;
- improve access for people who use assistive input, speech-to-text, localized terminology, informal language, or typo-tolerant interfaces;
- support multilingual participation without forcing every user-facing environment into one language or cultural convention;
- make AI-assisted actions easier to inspect by separating interpretation, consent, execution, and verification;
- preserve accountability while minimizing unnecessary disclosure of legal identity or sensitive personal information;
- reduce dependence on any single AI vendor, repository forge, operating system, or hosted service;
- make software, documentation, and digital records easier to preserve across technological and institutional change; and
- give individuals and organizations more practical control over local data, reproducible environments, and known-good software states.

## Accountability and trust

OSWAP does not assume that openness automatically creates trust. The project instead emphasizes inspectable provenance, explicit boundaries, deterministic validation, and independent verification so trust claims can be tested against records and system behavior.

## Accessibility and agency

Accessibility is treated as an architectural concern rather than a cosmetic feature. A system may accept flexible human expression while still requiring strict machine validation before execution. The intended result is greater human agency without converting probabilistic interpretation into unchecked authority.

## Preservation and institutional memory

Digital records can disappear through service shutdowns, policy changes, account loss, platform consolidation, incompatible formats, or simple neglect. OSWAP preservation and replication research therefore treats readable formats, verifiable copies, reproducible environments, and hosting independence as public-interest properties.

## Limits

These are intended impacts and design goals, not evidence that OSWAP has already achieved broad societal adoption or measured social outcomes. Claims about real-world impact should be supported by future deployments, accessibility testing, user research, independent review, and empirical evidence.
